# What's this?
So this is a cool table lamp with box to keep your pens and stuff with rechargeable battery. So when powercut is there you have an backup emergency lamp kinda also.
It uses an 1000mah lipo battery which shall long enough for around more than 2hrs+ and a type-c charger so that one can charge it back.
Your desk doesn't looks messy it also looks clean and good. 

# Why I made this?
Instead of making something high-tech which normally people won't even use I thought of making something that solves daily problems and issues powercuts during night.
Not much fancy but use wise it's alot helpful for many.
A cool warm white light not much bright not to dim just proper to keep the vibe right and area bright.
